Before the show

Installing the APK

Four steps, once — after the first install, updates from the same source won't ask again.

Open the link on your Android phone

Use the download button above. If you received the link elsewhere, open it in Chrome for the smoothest download.

Download the file from Drive

Tap the download icon in the top-right of the Drive preview screen to save the .apk to your device.

Allow installs from this source

Android will block it once and offer a settings link — tap it, then enable "Allow from this source" for your browser or Files app.

Open the file and install

Head back to your downloads, tap the .apk, and confirm install. MovieFlux will appear on your home screen.
